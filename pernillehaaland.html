Biography

Pernille Haaland is an award-winning Norwegian-British comedian, actor, and writer whose razor-sharp, unapologetically Nordic stand-up has played to sold-out audiences across 40 countries. Trained at the Royal Central School of Speech and Drama, her work spans international comedy festivals, television sketch writing, and West End theater productions. Known for her fearless cultural commentary and clinical comedic timing, she has established herself as one of Europe's sharpest comedic voices.
